Home Privacy Policy

BidHouse Group (BidHouse Saleroom Co., Ltd. and BidHouse Inc.) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.
Please read it together with the terms that govern each sale.

1. Personal Information We Collect
What We Collect

  1. Your name and contact details, including postal address, email address and phone number.
  2. Account and registration details when you register to bid or consign.
    • Bid history, purchase and consignment records, and payment information.
    • Identification documents where the law requires us to verify identity.
  3. Valuation and enquiry correspondence, including emails and call notes.
  4. Viewing and saleroom attendance records.
    • Preferences for sale categories and marketing communications.
    • Motor Cars consignment details, including vehicle documents.
    • Viewing appointments and event registrations.
  5. Website usage data collected through cookies (see Section 5).
  6. Survey responses and feedback you choose to provide.
  7. Records kept to meet our legal and accounting obligations.
  8. Information you provide when applying for a role with us.
  9. CCTV images recorded at our salerooms for security.
  10. Any other information you give us in the course of a sale.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only for the purposes below, and only as permitted by law.

3. Sharing Your Information
Who We Share With

We do not sell your personal information.
We share information only with the parties below, and only as needed to run our sales and meet our legal duties.

Where the law requires it, we may disclose information to courts, regulators and tax authorities.

Payment Providers Purpose
Card and bank payment processing and settlement. To provide information about our sales and services,
Used for surveys and client administration.
Delivery and logistics partners, for insured transport of sold lots. Insurers, for cover on consigned and sold property.
Professional advisers — lawyers, accountants and auditors.
Our saleroom specialists and valuers, in-house only. Group companies: BidHouse Inc.
Group companies: BidHouse Saleroom Co., Ltd. Our New York and Chicago salerooms.
Live online bidding platforms we operate. We never share your information for third-party marketing.
You may withdraw consent at any time (see Section 6). Sharing is limited to what each sale requires.
Sale partners acting on our instructions. Motor Cars consignors and buyers, as a sale requires.

4. Keeping Your Information Safe
Security & Retention

  1. Access to personal information is limited to staff who need it for their work.
  2. Records are kept only as long as a sale, account or legal duty requires.
  3. We review our security measures regularly and train all staff in data handling.
    • If a breach occurs, we will notify affected clients without undue delay.
    • Questions about security: contact the Privacy Manager (Section 6).
    • Paper records are held in locked storage at the Head Office.
  4. Electronic records are encrypted in transit and at rest.
  5. You may ask us to delete your information where the law allows.

5. Cookies & Analytics

  1. This site uses cookies to run the site and to understand how it is used.
  2. We use Google Analytics to measure visits and improve the site.
  3. You can refuse cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.
  4. Analytics cookies are anonymised before storage.
