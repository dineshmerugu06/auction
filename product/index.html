Flow From Consignment to Sale

More than selling —
With you at every step, from first call to final result.

  1. Consultation

    Contact us through this site or by phone — even at the earliest idea stage.
    Your BidHouse specialist listens first: we clarify your goals, your timeline and your priorities before a single lot is catalogued.

  2. Idea & Proposal

    Working with the Saleroom team, we assess each piece — condition, provenance, rarity and market — to propose the very best route to sale for you.
    Early estimates and market guidance are available at this stage.

  3. Research & Grading

    Each lot is examined in-house and graded by our specialists.
    Private viewings with our specialists are available on request.

  4. Estimate Confirmed

    Final estimates are confirmed in writing before the sale.
    Design of the Century

  5. Sale preparation and launch management

    We research, photograph and catalogue every lot, verifying condition and provenance before the sale.

  6. Live Sales

    Consistent quality, and results delivered on schedule.
    Post-sale follow-up and advice for your next consignment.
